Clip Studio

Clip Studio is a personal desktop tool for making short music videos (lyric clips and montages) and posting them to the creator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

It runs on the creator's own computer. When the creator links an account, Clip Studio uses the official TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google) APIs only to upload the videos the creator chooses to post, with the captions the creator writes, to that same creator's account.

What it does

  • Cuts and captions short vertical videos from the creator's own media.
  • Keeps a posting schedule for the creator's accounts.
  • Uploads a chosen video to a linked account when the creator clicks Post, or at the time the creator scheduled.
