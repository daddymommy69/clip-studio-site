Data deletion

Clip Studio keeps your data only on your own computer. To delete it:

  1. In Clip Studio, open Posting → ⚙ → Connections and click Unlink next to each account. This deletes that account's access tokens from your computer.
  2. Remove the app's access on each platform:
    • Instagram / Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations) → remove Clip Studio.
    • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → remove Clip Studio.
    • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/connections → Clip Studio → remove access.
  3. To remove everything else, delete the app's data folder on your computer.

Clip Studio has no server, so there is nothing stored anywhere else to delete. Questions: message @musicspac.e on TikTok
