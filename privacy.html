Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clip Studio ("the app") is a personal desktop application that runs on its user's own computer. It is used by its owner to post videos to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information the app accesses

When you link an account, the platform asks you to approve access. The app then receives:

  • TikTok: your basic profile (display name and account ID) and permission to upload videos to your account or to your drafts.
  • Instagram (Meta): the name and ID of your Instagram professional account and its connected Facebook Page, and permission to publish Reels to that account.
  • YouTube (Google): your channel name and ID and permission to upload videos to your channel.
  • Access tokens that let the app act on your behalf for the permissions above.

The app does not read your messages, followers, contacts, comments, analytics or any other account data, and it does not access accounts you have not linked.

How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the videos you choose to post, with the captions you write, to your own linked accounts, and to show you whether each post went through.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your own computer, in the app's local data folder. The app has no server of its own and does not send your information, tokens or videos anywhere other than directly to the platform you are posting to (TikTok, Meta or Google) through their official APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By linking a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Keeping or removing your data

Tokens are kept until you unlink the account in the app. You can delete everything at any time: see Data deletion. You can also remove the app's access from each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram/Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites; Google: the link above).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
